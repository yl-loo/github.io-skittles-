What to notice

  • At n = 14 the two distributions overlap heavily. A single packet with 1 or 2 yellows could easily have come from either simulation.
  • At n = 56 both distributions are narrower, so they overlap much less. A merged sample gives far clearer evidence about which world we are in.
  • The centres stay the same as n grows. Only the spread changes: it halves when the sample size is four times bigger.
