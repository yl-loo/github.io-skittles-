Yellow is the least popular Skittle colour (according to Skittles)

A news report says yellow (lemon) comes last among the five Skittles colours. If that is true, should we expect to find fewer yellow Skittles in a packet?

Read the article at foodandwine.com ↗

Our mission

We are going to simulate many samples of Skittles and look at how the proportion of yellow varies from sample to sample. Then we will use what we see to decide whether yellow really is the least common colour.
